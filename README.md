# 🎯 Lead Qualification & CRM Router

An intelligent lead management automation system built with **n8n** to streamline lead qualification, prioritize potential customers, and route leads to the appropriate CRM or sales workflow.

## 🚀 Project Overview

Businesses receive leads from different sources, but manually reviewing, qualifying, and organizing every lead takes time.

This project demonstrates how workflow automation can help process incoming leads, evaluate their potential, assign qualification statuses, and organize lead information for more efficient sales management.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📥 **Automated Lead Intake:** Collects lead information from configured forms or other supported sources.
- 🔍 **Lead Qualification:** Evaluates lead information against configured qualification criteria.
- 📊 **Lead Scoring:** Assigns scores to help prioritize potential customers, when configured.
- 🔀 **Smart Lead Routing:** Routes leads according to qualification results and workflow rules.
- 🗂️ **CRM Integration:** Organizes lead information in a connected CRM or database.
- 🏷️ **Lead Status Management:** Helps categorize leads for easier tracking and follow-up.
- ⚡ **Workflow Automation:** Reduces repetitive manual work in lead management.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **n8n** – Workflow automation and orchestration
- **Lead Intake Forms / Webhooks** – Receiving lead information
- **Data Processing** – Validating and organizing lead records
- **Conditional Logic** – Applying qualification and routing rules
- **CRM / Database Integration** – Storing and managing lead information

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Lead Capture:** The workflow receives lead information from a configured input source.
2. **Data Processing:** Incoming lead details are extracted and organized.
3. **Lead Qualification:** The lead is evaluated against predefined business criteria.
4. **Lead Scoring:** A score or priority level is assigned if scoring is configured.
5. **Conditional Routing:** The workflow directs the lead to the appropriate branch based on qualification results.
6. **CRM Management:** Lead information and status are stored in the connected system.
7. **Follow-up:** The workflow can trigger notifications or follow-up actions when configured.

## 🔄 Workflow Architecture

```text
Lead Form / Webhook
        ↓
   Lead Capture
        ↓
  Data Validation
        ↓
 Lead Qualification
        ↓
    Lead Scoring
        ↓
  Routing Conditions
        ↓
   CRM / Database
        ↓
 Notifications & Follow-up
```
